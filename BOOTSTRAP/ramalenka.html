About Ramalenka Village

Ramalenka is a beautiful and culturally rich village located near the Bay of Bengal. It is known for its lush green fields, serene lakes, and traditional festivals celebrated with great enthusiasm.

The community is actively involved in initiatives such as Swachha Bharat Mission, local sports tournaments, and annual Gram functions. Ramalenka has a vibrant social life, emphasizing education, culture, and sustainable development.

About Ramalenka Village

Ramalenka is a vibrant and culturally rich village with a population of approximately 2,430 residents, including 1,250 men and 1,180 women, maintaining a balanced sex ratio of 944 women for every 1,000 men. The village places a strong emphasis on education, boasting a literacy rate of 85% among men and 78% among women, which reflects the community’s commitment to learning and progress. Over the years, Ramalenka has developed into a model of inclusive growth, where both men and women actively participate in social, cultural, and developmental activities. The village thrives on its traditions and festivals, which bring the community together, while modern initiatives in health, education, and social welfare ensure a better quality of life for all residents. With a harmonious blend of cultural heritage and progressive outlook, Ramalenka continues to grow as a self-sustaining and inspiring village in the region.
